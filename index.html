Regional Anaesthesia Atlas

An interactive ultrasound-guided regional anaesthesia reference app, built for anaesthetists at North Devon District General Hospital and intended for integration into a Residents app in development.

While we wait, it is released as a standalone app for those with a desire to use AnSo content in portrait mode, or who have older devices that no longer support apps released in the app stores.
